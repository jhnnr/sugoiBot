# Economy Module

Economy system with balance, daily rewards, and work.

## ⌨️ Slash Commands

### `/balance`

Checks your or another user's balance.

#### Options

- `target` (Optional): The user to check balance for

### `/coinflip`

Bet your coins on a coin flip!

#### Options

- `amount` (Required): Amount of coins to bet
- `side` (Required): Pick heads or tails

### `/daily`

Claims your daily reward.

### `/work`

Works to earn some coins.
